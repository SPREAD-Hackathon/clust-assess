Method & keys

Files

final_output_extended.csvYour plasmid pairs: Plasmid A, Plasmid B and DCJ are required. subcommunity_A / subcommunity_B give the subcommunity of each plasmid, and pairs with dcj_censored = True are left out. Optional containment and mash (Mash distance) columns are drawn as their own pairwise heatmaps but are not part of the judgement. community_DCJ and community_containment are ignored.
inputs.tsv (optional)The pipeline's table of inputs. Each input's nearest database plasmid (nearest_db_plasmid, nearest_db_dcj, nearest_db_containment) is added as an input-to-reference pair when that plasmid is in the reference. Those known plasmids also place the input in their subcommunity.
Reference databasePart of the report builder (html_report/reference): the pling pair table of a subcommunity (plasmid_1, plasmid_2, subcommunity, dcj). Its pairs are the yardstick and their mean DCJ is the baseline. Pairs of a subcommunity that is not in it are not assessed.
Pair typesA pair with exactly one plasmid in the reference is a measured input–reference pair: it is shown in the network and the plasmid tables and is not part of the judgement. All other pairs are judged against the reference.

How a pair is judged

Closer than X%The share of the reference pairs of its subcommunity that are farther apart (ties count half). 50% is typical for the reference; above 50% is closer.
✔ Outbreak-relatedDCJ at or below the reference mean (or the chosen percentile or fixed threshold).
✖ UnrelatedDCJ above it. About half of all pairs would qualify as Outbreak-related even if the input were no different from the reference, so read the share together with Closer than reference.

Optional columns

subcommunity_A / _B (or community)The subcommunity of each plasmid (for example 7129 or community_0_subcommunity_7129). A pair whose plasmids are in different subcommunities is not assessed. When the file has none, it is taken from known reference plasmids in the file, or from the only subcommunity in the reference. When the file names subcommunities that are not in the reference, they are not assessed (you can choose to judge them against the reference anyway).
Plasmid A / B countryLocation of each plasmid. A single country column is applied to both. Also: geography, location, region.
Plasmid A / B hostHost of each plasmid, or one host column. Also: species, organism.
Plasmid A / B dateISO date (2024-05-14) or year, or one date column. Also: collection_date, year.

Intervals and checks

All intervals are 95% percentile-bootstrap intervals. Plasmids are resampled (1000 times, seeded so results are reproducible) and each pair is weighted by how many copies of its two plasmids are drawn, because pairs sharing a plasmid are not independent.

A subcommunity that is missing from the reference, or whose reference has fewer than 10 pairs, is not assessed and is listed in the Summary. A reference whose 95th-percentile DCJ is above 25 is flagged as broad.
